About the group 关于课题组

Waste into resources变废为宝，绿色催化

The Green Catalysis for Waste-to-X Lab, led by Prof. Yaxuan Jing at the Institute of Environmental Health, Nanjing University, focuses on the high-value utilization of waste and misplaced carbon resources — including plastics, biomass and emerging contaminants.

南京大学环境与健康研究院"绿色催化与资源循环"课题组由景亚轩副教授领衔，聚焦废弃塑料、生物质、新污染物等废弃/错位碳资源的高值化利用。

By designing advanced catalytic materials and green reaction processes, and breaking disciplinary boundaries, we conduct multi-dimensional frontier research — from theory and technology to engineering — converting waste carbon resources into fuels, important chemicals and advanced functional materials.

通过设计先进催化材料与绿色反应工艺，突破学科专业边界，将废弃/错位碳资源转化为燃料、重要化学品和先进功能材料，开展理论、技术、工程等多维度的绿色低碳前沿研究。

Group Culture课题组文化

Science happens between experiments, too. A look at life in our lab.

科研不仅在实验台前。看看我们实验室的日常。

Our group encourages free exploration and embraces the philosophy of "academic freedom, joyful research". We tailor each student's research direction to their interests and future plans, hold regular team-building activities, and foster a harmonious, collaborative and intellectually vibrant environment.

课题组鼓励自由探索，倡导"学术自由，快乐科研"的理念，结合每位学生的科研兴趣和未来规划定制合适的课题方向。课题组定期举办团建，营造科研环境和谐、学术气息浓厚、团队协作进步的良好氛围。
